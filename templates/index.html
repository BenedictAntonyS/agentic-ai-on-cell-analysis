🧬 Deep Learning GAN Architecture & Occlusion Sensitivity

Trained for 200 Epochs on the benchmark LIVECell dataset of SH-SY5Y Neuroblastoma label-free phase-contrast micrographs.

Generator Pipeline:

• Input Tensor: (Batch, 4, 128, 128, 1) representing 4 consecutive time-lapse steps.
• Architecture: Spatiotemporal feature extraction via ConvLSTM2D units, decoded via Conv2D layers with sigmoid activations.
• Output: Synthesized horizon mask (Batch, 128, 128, 1).

Occlusion Sensitivity Breakdown:

Occlusion ablation studies in notebook Cell 1 demonstrated that Frame 1 accounts for ~35.8% of output fidelity, because the initial frame establishes the foundational colony topology and centroid coordinates upon which local velocity vectors are propagated.
